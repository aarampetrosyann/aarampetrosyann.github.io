Kakuro

A number crossword. Fill the grid with digits 1 to 9 so every run adds up to its clue.

Grid size
Difficulty

How to play

  1. Fill every white cell with a digit from 1 to 9.
  2. Each clue is the sum of the run of cells to its right (across) or below it (down).
  3. A digit can only appear once in each run.
